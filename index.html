Object identification is ready. Choose a photo and select Identify Object.

Object recognition uses a free MobileNet model in your browser (internet needed to download it). It predicts from a limited set of categories and can be wrong. Do not use it to identify medicine, hazards, allergens, or emergencies.

Choose a photo, then ask Athena to read its printed words.

Free Athena uses in-browser OCR and simple local rules, not cloud AI. OCR resources download from a public CDN when first used; no OpenAI API key is needed. It cannot reliably identify objects or understand complex scenes. Verify important details.

OCR is performed in your browser using Tesseract.js. It may misread handwriting, unusual fonts, or blurry photos. Always verify important information.

08 / ATHENA LIFE TOOLS

Your independence toolkit

✦

Practical tools that work alongside Vision, Quest, Mail, and Athena Voice.

🆘 Emergency SOS

Save a trusted contact phone number. ECHO opens your device's calling or messaging app; it never automatically calls or sends a message. In an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services directly.

⏰ Smart reminders

Create reminders for tasks, appointments, or routines. Alerts run while ECHO is open; browser notifications may require permission and may not work on iPhone. These are not reliable for critical medication reminders.

📄 Document reader

Use Athena Vision to scan a printed letter, menu, form, or document. You can correct recognized text and hear it aloud.

OCR is performed in-browser. Verify important details; this is not a PDF document parser.

♿ Accessibility profiles

Save named sets of text size, contrast, motion and speech speed on this device. Profiles are local, not securely isolated by Google account.

🏅 Odyssey achievements

Earn badges for using ECHO and completing Quest steps. Achievements are calculated locally from your saved journey and actions on this device.
